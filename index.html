  • 2026
    Design stage

    Digital Well-being a mental-health check-in for my university

    After two students died within a single month in late 2025, my university responded by setting up a well-being society where students can talk to teachers and a psychiatrist. I noticed that almost nobody goes. Walking into that office is a public act, and for many students that is the hardest part.

    So I led the design of a first step that needs no office: a private check-in a student opens on their phone from a QR code on a noticeboard. It asks 37 questions built on the PHQ-9 and GAD-7 screening questionnaires, then either suggests self-help or recommends a visit to the society. It screens and never diagnoses, and any sign of suicidal thoughts skips the model and shows crisis help at once.

    Done so far: the questionnaire with the evidence behind each question, and a benchmark of three models on a public student-depression dataset, trained on CPU and on GPU. The app is not built and no student data has been collected. That waits for ethics approval and supervision by the society’s counsellors.

    Python, scikit-learn, XGBoost, RAPIDS cuML
